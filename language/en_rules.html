Night Cup Tournament Rules

Rules of the game

* The standard rules of handball will apply, with the exception of the rules regarding playing time.

* The matches are started and stopped by the match officials' table.

* If the playing time is less than 7 minutes, players shall not be sent off but must sit out the next match.

* Hvis spiller må stå over neste kamp, kan laget fremdeles spille med 7 spiller.



Night Cup Rules

Gjennomføring

* Before a tournament, the start time and the scheduled end time must be established.

* The matches will be played continuously until the scheduled end time of the tournament.

* If the time remaining until the tournament's end time is less than 50% of a match's duration, the match shall not be started.
   If this is not the case, the match must be played to completion, even if the scheduled end time is exceeded.

* At the start of the tournament, the first two teams on the list shall face each other, playing for the duration scheduled for the tournament.

* When the match is over, the winning team must remain on the court and face the first team on the list. The losing team
   comes last on the list. In the event of a draw, both teams will be placed at the bottom of the list (with the away team
   at the top) and two new layers are retrieved from the list (the layer that is at the top of the list becomes the home team).

* In principle, the matches are to be played continuously, and the time between matches is to be kept to a minimum.

* No team may play more than 3 consecutive matches. If a team has played 3 matches, then after the third match it must
   had to go back on the list, but the team will be placed above the losing team on the list.



RESULTS

* The tournament is not based on the most matches won, but on the most minutes played on the court.

* Both the losing and the winning teams have minutes played recorded for a match.

* In cases where two teams have played the same number of minutes upon completion of the tournament, the team that has scored the most
   goals scored in the tournament take precedence on the results list.
